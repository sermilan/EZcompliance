# Background and overview

## 背景和概述

## The growth of the digital economy

## 数字经济的增长

Data is the fuel of the new economy, and even more so of the economy to come. The world's most valuable resource is no longer oil, but data. It is estimated that the digital economy today is worth almost $3 trillion.

数据是新经济更是未来经济的燃料。世界上最有价值的资源不再是石油，而是数据。据估计，今天的数字经济价值近3万亿美元。

The digital economy is the economic activity that results from billions of everyday online connections among people, businesses, devices, data, and processes. The backbone of the digital economy is hyperconnectivity which means growing interconnectedness of people, organisations, and machines that results from the internet, mobile technology and the internet of things (IoT). Such an economy is characterised by advanced manufacturing, robotics and factory automation, new sources of data from mobile and ubiquitous internet connectivity, cloud computing, big data analytics, and artificial intelligence.

数字经济是由人、企业、设备、数据和流程之间的每天数十亿次的在线连接所产生的经济活动。数字经济的支柱是超高度连接，这意味着互联网、移动技术和物联网(IoT)带来的人、组织和机器之间的互联性日益增长。这种经济的特点是有先进的制造业、机器人和工厂自动化、来自移动和无处不在的互联网连接的新数据源、云计算、大数据分析和人工智能。

The growth of digital economy is taking place at an unprecedented speed and scale. It is predicted that by 2022, 60% of global GDP will be digitised with growth in every industry driven by digitally-enhanced offerings, operations, and relationships. Additionally, the World Economic Forum predicts that some 60 70% of new value will be "based on datadriven digitally enabled networks and platforms". Half the world's population is online, a third is on a social network. Mobile internet has penetrated 53% of the online population with digital advertising amounting to $170 billion. If ASEAN has the right data connectivity infrastructure and enablers in place, it has potential to create 35 smart cities by 2025 across 10 countries.

数字经济正以前所未有的速度和规模发展。据预测，到2022年，全球60%的GDP将来自由数字化增强的产品、运营和关系所驱动的各行业数字化增长。此外，世界经济论坛预测，约60-70%的新价值将“基于数据驱动的数字化网络和

Powered by a large, growing, and incredibly engaged internet user base, it is estimated that the ASEAN internet economy has reached $72 billion in gross merchandise value (GMV) in 2018 across online travel, eCommerce, online media, and ride hailing, and is on track to exceed $240 billion by 2025, $40 billion higher than previously estimated. ## Data Management Framework as a key for boosting digital economy

## 数据管理框架是推动数字经济的关键

Due to the growing interactions between data, connected things and people, trust in data has become the precondition for fully realising the gains of digital transformation. SMEs are threading a fine line between balancing digital initiatives and concurrently managing data protection and customer privacy safeguards to ensure that these do not impede innovation. Therefore, there is a motivation to focus on digital data governance as it is critical to boost economic integration and technology adoption across all sectors in the ten ASEAN Member States (AMS).

由于数据、联网物和人之间的互动越来越多，对数据的信任已经成为充分实现数字转型收益的先决条件。中小企业正在平衡数字驱动与同步管理数据保护和客户隐私保护之间划定适合界限，以确保不会阻碍创新。因此，我们有动力关注数字数据治理，因为这对于促进十个东盟成员国(AMS)所有部门的经济一体化和技术应用至关重要。

To ensure that their data is appropriately managed and protected, organisations need to know what levels of technical, procedural and physical controls they need to put in place. The categorisation of datasets help organisations manage their data assets and put in place the right level of controls. This is applicable for both data at rest as well as data in transit. The establishment of an ASEAN Data Management Framework will promote sound data governance practices by helping organisations to discover the datasets they have, assign it with the appropriate categories, manage the data, protect it accordingly and all these while continuing to comply with relevant regulations. Improved governance and protection will instil trust in data sharing both between organisations and between countries, which will then promote the growth of trade and the flow of data among AMS and their partners in the digital economy.

为了确保其数据得到适当的管理和保护，组织需要知道他们应实施何种级别的技术、程序和物理控制。数据集的分类有助于组织管理其数据资产，并设置适当的控制级别。这适用于静态数据和传输中的数据。东盟数据管理框架的建立将通过帮助组织发现他们拥有的数据集、为其配备适当的类别、管理数据、相应地保护数据以及所有这些，同时持续遵守相关法规，来促进健全的数据治理实践。改善治理和保护将在组织之间和国家之间建立对数据共享的信任，这将促进东盟国家及其数字经济伙伴之间的贸易增长和数据流动。

The ASEAN Data Classification Framework has been renamed as the ASEAN Data Management Framework(hereafter referred to as the "DMF") to recognise that companies need to develop data governance structure and appropriate data protection safeguards based on the purpose of data use throughout the data lifecycle (e.g. data in transmission, data at rest). Categorisation is a key precursor for three out of six foundational components of the DMF, which will be elaborated in the following sections. Categorisation guides downstream decisions in safeguarding data with the appropriate level of access and protection. It is an approach to identify, assess, manage, and protect data with appropriate protection measures. To achieve this, understanding the value of information and the protection measures designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information are crucial. To support the categorisation process, organisations must also have adequate policies and procedural documents, and established governance and oversight function along with monitoring and continuous improvement of their data management practices.

东盟数据分类框架已改名为东盟数据管理框架（以下简称"DMF"），以确认公司需要在整个数据生命周期（如传输中的数据、静止数据）中，根据数据使用目的制定数据治理结构和适当的数据保护保障措施。分类是DMF六个基础组件中三个组件的关键前提，将在以下章节阐述。分类指导下游以适当的访问和保护级别来保障数据的决策。它是一种通过适当的保护措施来识别、评估、管理和保护数据的方法。要做到这一点，了解信息的价值以及为保护信息的保密性、完整性和可用性而设计的保护措施至关重要。为了支持分类过程，各组织还必须有适当的政策和程序文件，建立治理和监督功能，同时监测和不断改进其数据管理做法。

# Gain and retain stakeholder trust while unlocking the potential for innovation

# 获得并保持利益相关者的信任，同时释放创新的潜力

The DMF has been developed to support the Data Life Cycle & Ecosystemstrategic priority. It is aimed at helping all businesses operating in ASEAN participate in the digital economy and practice data governance across all data types within an organisation throughout the data lifecycle and consider adequate protection for different types of data. With a DMF, organisations would be better equipped to protect data and instil trust and confidence in their customers and organisations they interact with, while the data is being leveraged for business innovation purposes.

DMF 是为了支持数据生命周期和生态系统战略重点而开发的，旨在帮助所有在东盟运营的业务参与数字经济，并在整个数据生命周期中对组织内的所有数据类型进行数据治理，并考虑对不同类型的数据进行充分保护。有了 DMF，各组织就能更好地保护数据，并在数据被用于商业创新目的的同时，向其客户和与之互动的组织灌输信任和信心。

---

The process of valuing information is a fundamental starting point for the development of a positive security culture across businesses. Businesses working with data have an obligation to respect the information they create, access and use, and are accountable for safeguarding these information assets.

评估信息的过程是贯穿业务中发展积极的安全文化的基本出发点。使用数据的企业有义务尊重他们创建、访问和使用的信息，并对保护这些信息资产负债。

In developing the framework, we learned that the initial name did not fully represent the entire process of enabling organisations in their own data management journey. It would involve more than just classifying data, as organizations would need to discover the data they have; protect it accordingly; monitor the data they hold; and comply with relevant regulations. As such, the revised name "Data Management Framework" is proposed to encapsulate the intended outcome set out in the Digital Data Governance initiative through this framework's 6 foundational components.

在开发框架的过程中，我们了解到最初的名称并不能完全代表组织在其自身数据管理的全过程。它涉及的不仅仅是数据的分类，因为组织需要发现他们所拥有的数据，对其进行相应的保护，监控他们所持有的数据，并遵守相关的法规。因此，修订后的名称“数据管理框架”旨在通过该框架的6个基本组成部分来概括数字数据治理计划中提出的预期结果。

One of the four strategic priorities identified in the ASEAN Framework on Digital Data Governance with the objective of data governance throughout the data lifecycle (e.g., collection, use, access, storage) and adequate data protection for different types of data.

东盟数字数据治理框架中确定的四个战略重点之一，目标是在整个数据生命周期(例如，收集、使用、访问、存储)进行数据治理，并为不同类型的数据提供充分的数据保护。

Regardless of whether the data is processed or stored in onpremise systems or in the cloud, according the data appropriate protection measures is the starting point for maintaining confidentiality, integrity and availability of the data.

无论数据是在本地系统还是云端进行处理或存储，根据数据采取适当的保护措施是保持数据机密性、完整性和可用性的起点。

## 适用范围

1. The DMF is designed to provide voluntary and nonbinding guidance based on best practices in the area of data management for businesses within AMS. The content should not be read as nor constitute as legal advice, nor construed as a tool for compliance to any law and regulations.

1. DMF 旨在根据数据管理领域的最佳做法，为东盟成员国内的企业提供自愿的、不具约束力的指导。该内容不应被理解为法律建议，也不应被解释为遵守任何法律法规的工具。

2. The DMF is intended to be adapted to varying business needs for adoption and tailoring by the businesses to their own systems of managing data.

2.DMF 旨在适应不同的业务需求，以便企业采用和定制自己的数据管理系统。

3. "Data" as used in this DMF refers to all data a business creates, collects, accesses, processes and transfers. This may include personal data and business transactional data.

3. DMF 中使用的“数据”是指企业创建、收集、访问、处理和传输的所有数据。这可能包括个人数据和商业交易数据。

4. The DMF is intended for the use of all private sector businesses operating in any ASEAN member state (AMS), including small and medium enterprises.

4. DMF 旨在供在任何东盟成员国经营的所有私营部门企业使用，包括中小型企业。

# 6 foundational components of the DMF

## DMF 的 6 个基本组成部分

## Overarching considerations when categorising and protecting data

## 分类和保护数据时的首要考虑

Companies have the flexibility in categorising their own datasets, appropriate to their respective needs and sectors. The areas for consideration listed here aim to assist businesses in assessing the sensitivity and value of the dataset and in establishing the risk-based controls.

公司可以根据各自的需求和行业，灵活地对自己的数据集进行分类。这里列出的考虑方面旨在帮助企业评估数据集的敏感性和价值，并建立基于风险的控制。

As controls should be applied based on the categorisation of dataset, businesses can keep these considerations in mind when performing the impact and risk assessment so as to assign the appropriate tiers, avoiding over protection of datasets that may hinder use of data for business purposes.

由于应根据数据集的分类进行控制，因此企业在进行影响和风险评估时，可牢记这些考虑因素，以便配备适当的层级，避免对数据集的过度保护而妨碍数据的商业用途。

The areas to consider are the following:

需要考虑的方面如下：

1、Nature and type of services provided: Consider the sector in which the organisation operates in and the service provided, in order to understand and take into account the usage of the information in possession of the organization (e.g., purpose for the information collection, methods of use, target customer).

---

所提供服务的性质和类型:考虑组织运营所在的领域和所提供的服务，以便理解和考虑组织所掌握的信息的使用情况(例如，信息收集的目的、使用方法、目标客户)。

2、Regulation: Consider current personal data legislations within AMS (e.g., PDPA, GDPR) and the sector / industry regulations if applicable (e.g., banking regulations and healthcare regulations).

法规: 考虑东盟成员国内现行的个人数据立法(如PDPA、GDPR)和适用的部门/行业条例(如银行条例和保健条例)。

3、Competitive landscape: The currency of the information (e.g., Patents, R&D)

竞争格局:信息的流通(如专利、研发)

4、Cost of safeguards vs. risk appetite: Consider the aggregate amount of risk regarding to data breach, that the organisation is able to accept in pursuit of its strategic goals and objectives. Furthermore, it is needed to consider the following aspects:

保障措施的成本与风险承受能力:考虑组织在追求其战略目标和宗旨时能够接受的数据违规风险总量。此外，需要考虑以下几个方面:

数据集中信息的汇总(例如，文件/系统表格和/或报告中的数据组合);

(2)The volume of data contained in a particular dataset, in most of the cases the greater

某一特定数据集所包含的数据量，在大多数情况下，数据量越大越好。

the number of data lost / disclosed without authorization the greater the impact or people affected (e.g., 1 day's transaction history vs 1 year's transaction history).

未经授权而丢失/披露的数据数量越多，影响越大，受影响的人员也越多(例如，1天的交易历史与1年的交易历史)。

5、Customer expectations: Consider the data subject (e.g., high profile individuals, minors) and the contractual obligations defined on data protection safeguards to apply.

### 4. Impact / risk assessment

### 4. 影响/风险评估

## Assess the categories

## 评估类别

After establishing the data inventory, the next step for the organisation is to tailor its own thresholds for categorising data and to determine the assignment of appropriate category to the dataset by assessing the impact to the organisation if the dataset be compromised.

建立数据清单后，组织的下一步是定制自己的数据分类阈值，并通过评估数据集受损对组织的影响来确定配备数据集的适当类别。

The key element to carry out this impact / risk assessment is the categorisation matrix. The categorisation matrix provides guidelines and thresholds to be able to assess the impact on individuals and on the business environment within an organisation.

进行这种影响/风险评估的关键要素是分类矩阵。分类矩阵提供了指导方针和阈值，以便能够评估对个人和组织内业务环境的影响。

Any proposed categorisation matrix should outline appropriate impact categories with broad expectations and provide clear guidance on the tiers to assign the dataset based on it risk impact level for each impact category. Moreover, this categorisation matrix should be defined in a way that allows organizations to easily apply the right categories, marking and handling, and for industries to selfregulate.

---

任何提议的分类矩阵都应概述具有广泛预期的适当影响类别，并提供关于各层的明确指导，以便根据每个影响类别的风险影响水平来配别数据集类别。此外，这种分类矩阵的定义应使各组织能够方便地应用正确的类别、标记和处理，并使各行业能够自我调节。

Given that this is not a prescriptive guideline, the number of tiers/categories depend on the business consideration and needs. The more the organisations want to granulate the protection measures, the more tiers/categories should be defined.

鉴于这不是一个规范性的指导方针，层级/类别的数量取决于业务考虑和需求。各组织越想细化保护措施，就应该定义越多的层级/类别。

Consider the impact when the data is compromised in the following three parameters are compromised:

考虑当数据在以下三个参数被泄露时的影响：

1、Confidentiality (C): Risk of unauthorised / inappropriate disclosure. For information to be confidential, the access to some information needs to be restricted because it could harm interests of the stakeholders.

保密性(C)：未经授权/不适当披露的风险。为确保信息的保密，需要限制某些信息的获取，因为这可能会损害利益相关者的利益。

2、Integrity (I): Risk to information quality / corruption. For information to be useful and serve the purpose, it must be accurate and complete.

完整性（I）:信息质量/被破坏风险。为了使信息有用并服务于目的，它必须准确和完整。

3、Availability (A): Risk to information not being available to intended users. For information to be useful and serve the purpose, it must be available when it is needed and, in a form that is able to be consumed by users.

可用性(A): 预期用户无法获得信息的风险。为了使信息有用并服务于目的，它必须在需要时可用，并且以能够被用户使用的形式。

---

财务:影响公司财务流程的风险(如会计和报告、税务等)。

2、Strategic: risks affecting achievement of the strategic objectives of the company (e.g., governance, strategic planning, major initiatives, etc.)

战略性:影响公司战略目标实现的风险(如治理、战略规划、重大举措等)。

3、Operational: risks affecting the operations of the organization (e.g., sales & marketing, supply chain, etc.)

运营风险:影响组织运营的风险(如销售和营销、供应链等)。

4、Compliance: risks affecting the company's compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., legal, code of conduct, etc.)

合规性:影响公司遵守监管要求的风险。(如法律、行为准则等)

# 5. Controls

### 5. 控制

## Protect the data

## 保护数据

The final step is to implement risk-based controls that are commensurate to the potential impact of the data being compromised. A control includes any procedure that is used and relied upon to prevent errors from occurring during data collection, use and/or disclosure processes (preventive controls) or to detect and correct errors that may have occurred in these processes mentioned above (detective controls).

最后一步是实施基于风险的控制，这些控制要与被泄露数据的潜在影响相称。控制措施包括任何用于防止在数据收集、使用和/或披露过程中发生错误的程序（预防控制），或用于检测和纠正上述过程中可能发生的错误（检测控制）。

A control involves taking an action to mitigate or manage the risk that data will be compromised and increase the probability that the organisation will achieve its intended purpose. The organisation needs to establish the risk tolerance, which determines the acceptable level of risk that they are willing to take on. Controls are then identified to sufficiently mitigate the risk and bring it within the risk appetite. Often times, the risk is not completely mitigated. The residual risks that exist after considering controls or other management activities will be assessed and accepted by management in a risk acceptance process.

控制措施涉及采取一项行动，以消减或管理数据泄露的风险，并增加组织实现其预期目的的可能性。组织需要建立风险承受能力，这决定了他们愿意承担的可接受的风险水平。然后确定控制措施，以充分降低风险，并将其控制在风险承受范围内。通常情况下，风险不会完全消减。在考虑到控制措施或其他管理活动后存在的剩余风险将在风险接受过程中由管理层进行评估和接受。

---

Employees should be encouraged to comply with appropriate technical, procedural and physical safeguards to ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of data at all stages of the data lifecycle. The technical safeguards compile the IT controls established within the IT application programs and environment, to protect the data within the organisations' systems. The procedural safeguards include the baseline, clarity and constraint or gap in design on how to protect the data. The physical safeguards are referred to the protection measures around the hard copy versions including data that confirms the limited access.

应鼓励员工遵守适当的技术、程序和物理保护措施，以确保数据在生命周期所有阶段的机密性、完整性和可用性。在信息技术应用程序和环境中建立的信息技术控制中融合技术性保障措施，以保护组织系统中的数据。程序性保障措施包括如何保护数据的基线、明确性和约束性或设计上的差距。物理性保障措施指的是围绕硬拷贝版本的保护措施，包括确认有限访问的数据。

数据生命周期是指数据从最初产生或获取到最终在其使用寿命结束时被删除所经历的一系列阶段。对数据的保护将根据数据生命周期的不同阶段(如收集、使用、传输中、静止、访问、存储)而有所不同。在设计这些保护措施时，还应根据业务性质和所提供的服务类型，考虑到数据的敏感性。

Having too many categories and/or overinclusion of data may lead to the organisation incurring additional costs when implementing controls due to additional resources required for securing, monitoring, measuring, remediating and reporting risks.

类别过多和/或过多纳入数据可能导致组织在实施控制时产生额外的成本，因为需要额外的资源来确保、监测、衡量、补救和报告风险。

Each organization, depending on its size or volume of information handled, may apply varying degrees of protection that it deems necessary. When regulations are not prescriptive, organisations should define their own categories and protection controls matrix to determine the appropriate level of protection tailored to their needs, organization's consideration, strategy and objectives. Stricter information handling requirements are often required for the transmission, storage and disposal of data that may present a significant negative impact on a customer or organisation if confidentiality, integrity and/or availability is compromised.

各组织根据其规模或所处理的信息量，可采用其认为必要的不同程度的保护措施。当法规没有规定时，各组织应确定自己的类别和保护控制矩阵，以定制适合的保护水平确保符合其所需、组织的考量、战略和目标。如果数据的保密性、完整性和/或可用性受到损害，可能会对客户或组织造成重大的负面影响，那么对于数据的传输、存储和处置往往需要更严格的信息处理要求。

监控、测量、分析和评估是保持基础部分更新和优化的关键活动。

组织必须考虑以下监控和持续改进活动：

---

data protection controls matrix, taking into account results of security audits, incidents, effectiveness measurements, suggestions and feedback from the business.

审查与每个类别相关的控制措施:定期审查为数据集配备类别中的流程设计，如目标、类别和数据保护控制矩阵，同时考虑到安全审计、偶发事件、有效性评价、业务建议和反馈。

通过定期测试对既定控制措施的设计和运行有效性进行审查，以支持组织对数据管理的持续改进，特别是第五个基本组成部分“控制措施”。

## Conclusion

## 结论

The growth of the digital economy brings many opportunities for innovation across businesses. At the same time, the changing regulatory and threat landscape increases the need for appropriate data protection measures. Lack of clarity on the kind of data and the manner in which data can be shared between businesses may also discourage businesses from leveraging the use of data to drive business growth. Businesses are working to balance between using data to create innovative products and services in the digital world while at the same time, protecting data adequately. On one hand, businesses face increasing pressure to preserve personally identifiable and business critical information and instil trust in data privacy amongst stakeholders. On the other hand, they are pressured to innovate business operations, products and services in order to survive and are rewarded for the use of data as a driver for digital economy. To overcome this, it is essential for ASEAN to establish common frameworks to support the sharing of best practices and equipping SMEs with the capabilities to unlock the value of data.

数字经济的增长为各行各业的创新带来了许多机会。同时，不断变化的监管和威胁环境增加了对适当数据保护措施的需求。企业之间对可以共享的数据种类和数据共享方式的不明确性，也可能会阻碍企业利用数据来推动业务增长。企业正在努力平衡在数字世界中使用数据创造创新产品、服务与充分保护数据之间的关系。一方面，企业面临着越来越大的压力，需要保护个人身份和业务关键信息，并在利益相关者中灌输对数据隐私的信任。另一方面，为了生存，他们面临着创新业务运营、产品和服务的压力，并因使用数据作为数字经济的驱动力而获得回报。为了克服这一点，东盟必须建立共同框架，支持分享最佳做法，使中小企业具备释放数据价值的能力。
