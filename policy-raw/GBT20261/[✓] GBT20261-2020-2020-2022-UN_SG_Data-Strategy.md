### Introduction /介绍

In the past, generating value from data was often seen as an isolated concern for experts. Today, data and analytics use cases permeate virtually everything we do, everywhere. Everyone will participate in a data-driven transformation, grounded in UN values and human rights. 在过去，从数据中产生价值往往被视为专家们的专利。如今，数据和分析用例几 乎渗透到我们所做的一切中，无处不在。每个人都将参与以联合国价值观和人权 为基础的数据驱动的转型。

### Data is not an isolated concern, but permeates our organization and its use is integral to our success. 数据并不是一个孤立的问题，而是渗透到我们的组织中，数据的使用是我们成功不可 或缺的一部分。

**IN SUMMARY /总结**

* In the past, generating value from data was often an isolated concern for IT, operations, geospatial or statistics experts. Today, data is pervasive and analytics tasks permeate the organization. They are integral to our success.

● 在过去，从数据中产生价值往往是 IT、运营、地理空间或统计专家关注的问 题。如今，数据无处不在，分析任务渗透到组织中。它们是我们成功不可或缺的 一部分。

* Simultaneously, data use is increasingly specific to each pillar and

function, requiring more specialization, agility, and governance.

● 同时，数据将应用于每个主题和职能，需要更多的专业化、敏捷性和治理。

* This requires a new set of analytics and data management capabilities, supported by enablers, including people and culture, governance, partnerships and technology.

● 这就需要一套新的分析和数据管理能力，并由使能者者提供包括人员和文化、 治理、伙伴关系和技术等方面的支持。

* Not just IT teams, but the entire organization must get involved, as data is woven into everything we do. This is not simple.

● 不仅是 IT 团队，而且整个组织都必须参与进来，因为数据被“编织”到我们 所做的一切中。这并不简单。

* New analytics capabilities, such as deriving insights from predictive models, need to be nurtured across the organization.

● 需要在整个组织中培养新的分析能力，如从预测模型中获得洞见。

* Better data management capabilities, for example to integrate data from many sources, must be fostered in every pillar and function.

● 必须在每一个专题和职能中培养更好的数据管理能力，例如整合来自许多来 源的数据。

* Over time, new data abilities will also shape new innovative services and products.

● 随着时间的推移，新的数据能力也将塑造新的创新服务和产品。

* Adjusting to this reality implies a complex transformation, driven by

the realization that data is a shared strategic asset.

● 适应这一现实意味着一场复杂的转型，首先是意识的转变，即认识到数据是 一种共享战略资产。

* It also requires strategic discipline in capturing opportunities, navigating challenges and accounting for the complex tasks ahead.

● 它还要求在捕捉机会、应对挑战和应对未来复杂任务方面具有战略纪律。

● 理解广泛的结构化和非结构化数据，并将这些知识应用于分析、规划、交付 和评估。

● 提供实时见解，了解组织必须在何处采取行动来应对风险和发现新出现的机 会。

● 整合组织内部和跨组织的数据，以更有效地应对相互关联的需求。

● 基于复杂的数据模式，对潜在风险（无论是在危机中还是在采购中）进行预 测。

● 使用量身定制的及时信息设计仪表板，以衡量成功和推动战略。

● 比基于静态历史报告的传统技术更有效地预测结果。

● 确定机会，以更好地支持利益相关者，提供更量身定制、更快速和增值的服 务、产品、项目或方案。

● 解决对资源和结果的更深层次透明度和及时问责的需求，包括远程环境中的

### Challenge: Using data well is a core competency for all organizations in the 21st century, but we are not yet prepared for it .

**挑战：利用好数据是 21 世纪所有组织的核心能力，但我们还没有准备好。**

**IN SUMMARY /总结**

* Managing, using & sharing data will be integral to our success in the years ahead, but we are not yet equipped to treat data as a shared strategic asset & create optimal value.

● 管理、使用和共享数据将是我们在未来几年取得成功的不可或缺的一部分， 但我们还没有能力将数据视为共享战略资产并创造最佳价值。

* With greater capacity to create, manage and use data, many colleagues already make better decisions and, ultimately, better serve people and planet. Yet, this happens in siloes.

● 随着创建、管理和使用数据的能力的提高，许多同事已经做出了更好的决策， 最终更好地服务于人类和地球。然而，这种情况还只是在局部中发生。

* Currently, our data is often acquired, stored and used for a single purpose within pillars or functions. Access is often difficult, partly because of a lack of awareness the data could help others or reticence to share what we can.

● 目前，我们的数据通常是在职能或层级中获取、存储和用于单一目的的。访

问往往很困难，部分原因是缺乏意识，数据可能会帮助他人，或者拒绝分享我们

所能分享的东西。

* Compounding these challenges is a lack of cross-cutting data governance and senior engagement to provide strategic direction, set data priorities and drive cultural change.

● 使这些挑战更复杂的是，缺乏跨领域的数据治理和高层参与，以提供战略方 向、确定数据优先事项和推动文化变革。

* In addition, uneven practices in data management, combined with data quality gaps, also limit our ability to harness machine learning responsibly and create new risks.

● 此外，数据管理方面的不平衡做法，加上数据质量差距，也限制了我们负责 任地利用机器学习的能力，并可能带来新的风险。

* Many UN organizations have pursued greater availability of data by investing in open and shared data, but more can be done to raise the volume of what is available by default.

● 许多联合国组织通过投资于开放和共享数据，寻求更多的数据可用性，但为 此也付出了很多的重复劳动。

* In the future, an integrated whole-of-UN approach must enable more synergy through sharing and interoperability; encourage openness and learning; and foster the spread of common standards and best practice. With this strategy, we are at an inflection point.

● 今后，整个联合国的综合办法必须通过共享和互操作性实现更多的协同作用；

鼓励开放和学习；促进公共标准和最佳实践的传播。期望这个战略将成为拐点。

### SOME OF OUR CHALLENGES /我们的一些挑战

* UNEVEN data cultures, skills and capabilities lead to sub-optimal decisions, strategies, resourcing and impact

● 不均衡的数据文化、技能和能力导致次优的决策、战略、资源和影响

* INADEQUATE technology environments frustrate colleagues & partners in collecting, discovering and accessing data they need

● 不理想的技术环境会导致同事和合作伙伴在收集、发现和访问他们所需的数 据时感到沮丧

● 缺乏横向治理会造成数据孤岛，阻碍协作，并影响数据质量和使用

● 不完善的个人数据保护和隐私原则，加上新技术，会带来新的风险

* LIMITED systems integration generates duplication, errors and extra work within and across organizations

● 有限的系统集成会在组织内部和组织之间产生重复、错误和额外工作

* MISALIGNED standards usage impedes not only system-wide data aggregation & interoperability, but also gender disaggregation

● 错误的标准使用不仅阻碍了系统范围的数据聚合和互操作性，而且阻碍了性

别细分

* LIMITED data and analytics use in products and services degrades usability, relevance and impact

● 产品和服务中有限的数据和分析使用会降低可用性、相关性和影响

### Designing our strategy /设计我们的战略

To build organizational capabilities and enablers for everyone’s data success, we will pursue a problem-driven approach that starts with concrete use cases that add value for organization, people and planet, grounded in strategic foundations that reflect our UN values and ambitions. 为了建设组织能力和推动人人数据成功的因素，我们将采取**问题驱动的方法**，从 具体的用例开始，为组织、人员和地球增加价值，并以反映我们联合国价值观和 雄心壮志的战略基础为基础。

Our strategy is inspired by a vision of a world where people and planet get the support they need in the moments that matter most. ### Principles: 12 basic principles to guide data action by everyone, everywhere in the UN family – for insight, impact & integrity. 原则：12 项基本原则，指导联合国大家庭中每个人的数据行动——洞察力、影 响和完整性。

**DATA PRINCIPLES /数据原则**

* ASSET: We treat data and information as shared strategic assets and treat them with at least the same discipline as other recognized (tangible and intangible) assets are.

● 资产：我们将数据和信息视为共享战略资产，并至少以与其他公认（有形和 无形）资产相同的原则对待它们。

* EXCELLENCE: We strive for excellence and continuous improvement in how we generate value from data for the organization and the people we serve – focused on the most vulnerable and marginalized.

● 卓越：我们努力在如何从数据中为组织和我们所服务的人员创造价值方面取 得卓越和持续改进——重点是最脆弱和边缘化的群体。

* DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY: We ensure the protection and privacy of personal data in any form, processed in any manner, and exercise caution when processing data of vulnerable or marginalized individuals or groups.

● 数据保护和隐私：我们确保以任何形式、以任何方式处理的个人数据的保护 和隐私，并在处理弱势或边缘化个人或群体的数据时谨慎行事。

* AGENCY: We use data to augment human decision-making, not to fully replace it, and to positively contribute to peace and security, sustainable development, and human rights, with a focus on gender impact.

● 机构：我们利用数据来加强人类决策，而不是完全取代它，并积极促进和平

与安全、可持续发展和人权，重点是性别影响。

* FAIRNESS: Our data usage is responsible, impartial, and respects, protects and promotes human rights. This includes eliminating bias and not discriminating based on gender, race, religion or any other factor.

● 公平：我们的数据使用是负责任的、公正的，尊重、保护和促进人权。这包 括消除偏见，不基于性别、种族、宗教或任何其他因素的歧视。

* ACCOUNTABILITY: We have data governance in place to clarify data roles, responsibilities, standards and policies and to ensure accountability for data assets, insights and actions.

● 问责制：我们制定了数据治理，以澄清数据角色、责任、标准和政策，并确 保对数据资产、见解和行动的问责制。

* TRANSPARENCY: We manage our data and analytical products in a transparent manner by ensuring our outputs are comprehensible and traceable.

● 透明度：我们通过确保我们的产出可理解和可追溯，以透明的方式管理我们 的数据和分析产品。

* OWNERSHIP: We do not tolerate data hoarding. Data belongs to the organization (or is held in trust), not to teams or individuals. By default, data is openly available to colleagues unless there is a good reason for it to remain confidential.

● 所有权：我们不容忍数据囤积。数据属于组织（或托管），而不是团队或个人。 默认情况下，除非有充分的理由保持机密性，否则数据是公开向同事提供的。

* STEWARDSHIP: We assign data stewards at every level to nurture

quality, access, use, protection and other responsibilities for our data assets.

● 管理：我们在各级分配数据管理员，以培养数据资产的质量、访问、使用、 保护和其他责任。

* SECURITY: We make sure our data is secure and that its usage is safe.

● 安全：我们确保我们的数据是安全的，并确保其使用是安全的。

* INVENTORY: We catalogue, describe and classify our data assets in inventories, using common standards where possible, so that their characteristics, value and sensitivity are readily accessible at any time.
